Now I personally play Genshin Impact every single days. I get on about 3-5 times a day. I find it attracting to do my daily quest. Seeing the characters I spend lots of money and hours building, it brings me a sense of satisfaction. On top of everything, it is a great time killer. Don't even remember just how many hours I have wasted on the game already. HAHAHAHAHAHHA.

With that all being said, I have also started to realize that playing this much might not always be a good thing. While the game gives me enjoyment and a sense of achievement, it also takes up a lot of my time and attention. Sometimes I log in out of habit instead of actually wanting to play. The daily system makes it feel like I have to complete tasks so I don't miss rewards, which can turn the game into a responsibility instead of entertainment.

I think the important thing is finding a balance. Games like this are designed to keep players coming back every day, especially with daily quests and limited-time events. As long as I can control my time and spending, and make sure the game does not affect my school, work, or real life, then it can still be a fun hobby instead of a distraction.
